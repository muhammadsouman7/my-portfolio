About me

Hi, I'm Muhammad Souman, a full stack & AI developer. Most people call me Somy.

I'm drawn to clean, functional interfaces. Over four years I've built that skill in HTML, CSS, JavaScript and Bootstrap, turning rough ideas into websites and web apps people enjoy using.

Recently I moved into full stack development with Python and Flask, building the backend systems that carry a project from first commit to launch. I also build custom WordPress websites for clients who need a site they can manage themselves.

I'm studying for a B.S. in Artificial Intelligence at NUML, Islamabad. I'm in my 8th semester with a CGPA of 3.64, and it has changed how I approach problems and design AI features.

I'm also the CEO and co-founder of Septaneuron, a software company based in Rawalpindi, Pakistan.
